            <h2>Description</h2>
            <p>"5 Minutes Left" is an interactive choose-your-path game where players navigate through critical decisions with only five minutes to spare. The premise of the game is built around a high-stakes scenario where every choice impacts the outcome, making time management and decision-making essential.</p>
            <p>Players are presented with different situations and must choose from multiple options, each leading to different paths and consequences. The objective is to make the best decisions under pressure to reach a successful conclusion before time runs out. The game's narrative is designed to be immersive, with engaging storylines that keep players on the edge of their seats.</p>
            <p><strong>Key Features:</strong></p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Time-Restricted Decisions:</strong> Players have a limited amount of time to make their choices, adding urgency and excitement.</li>
                <li><strong>Multiple Endings:</strong> The game features various endings based on the decisions made, encouraging replayability to explore different outcomes.</li>
                <li><strong>Dynamic Storytelling:</strong> Each decision affects the storyline, creating a unique experience for every playthrough.</li>
                <li><strong>Engaging Scenarios:</strong> Players face challenging and thought-provoking scenarios that test their problem-solving skills and quick thinking.</li>
            </ul>
            <p>"5 Minutes Left" is a thrilling adventure combining strategy and storytelling, offering a captivating experience for players who enjoy high-pressure decision-making and interactive narratives.</p>
